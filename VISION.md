Product name: Personal AI Operating System (PAIOS)

Problem:
Users manage life through fragmented apps. Coordination cost is high. Automation is unsafe.

Solution:
A permissioned execution system that converts intent into controlled actions across apps.

Initial domains (locked):
- Gmail (read, draft)
- Google Calendar (read, create)
- Subscription detection via email (read-only)

Explicit non-goals:
- No autonomous actions
- No social media
- No health actions
- No payments
- No web browsing agents

Success metric:
User trusts the system to act on their behalf with confirmation.
